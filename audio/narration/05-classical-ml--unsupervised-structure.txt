Chapter 5: Classical machine learning and useful baselines. Unsupervised structure.

K-means assigns points to k centers and alternates assignment with center updates to reduce within-cluster squared distances. It assumes that this geometry is useful; clusters can be unstable, differently scaled, or socially meaningless. P C A compresses variance directions rather than discovering labels. Anomaly detection flags departures from a reference pattern; rare does not mean harmful, and harmful behavior can resemble common behavior.
