Chapter 19: Projects, mastery gates and interview practice. Rubric and leadership evidence.

Score each from 0 (absent and wrong) to 3 (clear, tested and defended): problem framing, mechanism and math, evaluation, implementation and debugging, capacity and cost, security and privacy, operations, tradeoffs and communication. A useful rehearsal target is at least 2 in every category, then strengthen weak areas; this is a study rubric, not an employer's hiring bar. A serious leakage or authorization error requires correction regardless of total score.

For leadership questions use only actual experience. Explain context, your specific decision, alternatives, evidence, outcome and what you would change. Do not invent achievements to match a role. Practice disagreement about quality targets, build-versus-buy, migration ownership and incident response. The senior signal is clear judgment under constraints, including knowing what evidence is missing.
