Chapter 8: Tokens, embeddings and transformers. One attention head, with shapes.

At each position, a query vector is compared with key vectors at permitted positions. After scaling and masking, softmax converts comparison scores to weights; those weights combine value vectors. Queries and keys control the weighting; values supply the content being mixed. These are learned calculations, not conscious searches.

The two-position example below will make that operation concrete. For its general notation, T counts positions and d sub model counts coordinates in each input vector. Let X be T by d sub model. Compute Q equals X times W sub Q, K equals X times W sub K and V equals X times W sub V. Q and K are T by d sub k; V is T by d sub v. Scores S equals Q times K transposed divided by square root of d sub k have shape T by T. Add a mask M with zero for allowed positions and negative infinity for prohibited ones. Row-wise softmax produces A; output is A times V with shape T by d sub v.

Queries and keys determine weights; values supply mixed content. The square root of d sub k scale controls typical score magnitude under a simplifying independent-unit-variance argument; it is not a probability correction. Multi-head attention computes multiple such projections and combines their outputs, allowing different learned interactions. See the original Transformer paper for the architecture and scaled attention definition.

Worked example: let the last query be (1,0), keys (1,0) and (0,1), values (2,0) and (0,4). d sub k equals 2, so scores are (zero point seven zero seven,0), softmax weights about (zero point six seven zero,zero point three three zero), and output about (1.340,1.321). These are weighted coordinates, not a retrieved sentence or proof of reasoning. At the first position in a causal decoder the second key must be masked; its weight becomes zero and output is exactly (2,0).

Now check the complete two-position calculation. Both rows of the query-weight matrix are one, zero. The key-weight matrix is the two-by-two identity, so it preserves each input coordinate. The value-weight matrix is diagonal, with two in the first position and four in the second. The resulting query matrix has rows one, zero and one, zero. The key matrix has rows one, zero and zero, one. The value matrix has rows two, zero and zero, four. Multiplying queries by transposed keys gives two identical score rows, each one, zero. Divide these scores by the square root of two. In the first row, mask the second position by assigning it negative infinity. The first row's softmax weights are therefore one, zero. The second row remains approximately zero point six six nine seven six two and zero point three three zero two three eight. Multiplying these attention weights by the values yields the first output row two, zero. The second output row is approximately one point three three nine five two three, one point three two zero nine five four. There are two rows and two output coordinates, so the output shape is two by two.
