Chapter 14: Training compute, hardware and inference serving. Introduction and prerequisites.

Prerequisites: 07 to 09 and 13. Outcomes: estimate memory and capacity with explicit assumptions, separate prefill from decode, and reason about parallelism and efficiency. Numeric estimates here are hypothetical arithmetic, not hardware benchmarks.

Will the model fit in memory, and will requests finish fast enough? Follow one request from prompt processing to generated tokens, then add other users and a queue. Hardware and distributed training come later as a deeper pass, after the capacity questions they help answer.
