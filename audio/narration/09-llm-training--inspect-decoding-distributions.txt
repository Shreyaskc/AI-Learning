The order is explicit because library conventions can differ. For logits [2,1,0] at tau equals 1, probabilities are approximately [zero point six six five two,zero point two four four seven,zero point zero nine zero zero]. Top-k equals 2 removes the last token and renormalizes to [zero point seven three one one,zero point two six eight nine,0]. Top-p equals zero point eight then retains both remaining tokens because the first alone has mass below zero point eight. Lower temperature sharpens scores; it does not add evidence or guarantee truth.

The helper rejects temperature zero instead of dividing by zero; greedy argmax is a separate limiting decision rule. Real runtimes can still vary under nominally deterministic settings through kernels, scheduling or provider changes.
