Question 2. Does high reward establish safety? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: only for what reward and evaluation actually measure, subject to gaming and generalization gaps.

Question 3. Why can lower pretraining loss coexist with worse user behavior? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: objectives, task distributions and output preferences differ.
