Question 1. X is 20 by 3; W is 3 by 7. What shape is XW? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: 20 by 7. W X is undefined because the inner dimensions differ.

Question 2. Why can raw Euclidean distance be misleading for age in years plus income in dollars? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: unit scale can dominate geometry. Standardization is one option, selected for the task and fit on train only.

Question 3. Derive d[(w times x minus y) squared] divided by dw. Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: 2(w times x minus y)x. Removing the one-half multiplies the gradient by two, which interacts with learning rate.

Question 4. A vector has zero norm. What should cosine code do? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: reject or define a documented fallback; dividing by zero has no mathematical cosine interpretation.
