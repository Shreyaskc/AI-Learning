Chapter 0: Start here: learn, build, explain. Follow the story.

First define a decision and the information available for it (01). Turn examples into numbers, express uncertainty and learn from error (02 to 05). Check whether the result works for the real decision (06). Then understand neural and language models (07 to 09), build evidence and controlled actions around them (10 to 13), and operate and challenge the system (14 to 18). Use projects and changing interview scenarios to demonstrate the learning (19).
