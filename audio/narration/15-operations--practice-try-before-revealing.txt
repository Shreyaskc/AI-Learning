Question 1. A new prompt passes every unit test but doubles output length. Is release safe? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: evaluate cost, latency, truncation, user usefulness and quality under realistic workload; unit tests cover only stated invariants.

Question 2. A drift alarm fires while labeled performance is stable. Retrain automatically? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: investigate cause and sample coverage first; retraining has risk and cost, and drift alone is not sufficient evidence.

Question 3. What belongs in a runbook? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: symptom, severity, owner and on-call, diagnostic queries, safe mitigation, rollback and kill-switch instructions, escalation and recovery verification.
