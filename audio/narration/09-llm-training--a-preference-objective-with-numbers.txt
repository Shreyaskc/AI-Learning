For a toy direct preference optimization pair, pi is the policy’s probability for a complete response, pi sub ref the frozen reference policy’s probability, and beta a positive strength parameter. Define the margin The margin m is beta times the chosen response log probability ratio between policy and reference, minus the corresponding log probability ratio for the rejected response and loss The negative natural logarithm of the sigmoid of margin m. A policy equal to its reference has margin zero and loss natural logarithm of 2 approximately zero point six nine three one four seven. With reference probabilities zero point five divided by zero point five, policy zero point eight divided by zero point two and beta equals zero point one, margin equals zero point one times natural logarithm of 4 approximately zero point one three eight six two nine and loss approximately zero point six two six two three three. The lab calculates this stably. The objective rewards relative preference under the pair; it does not prove the chosen answer is true. This is a scalar objective demonstration, not trained direct preference optimization or PPO.
