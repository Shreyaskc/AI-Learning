Question 1. Why can a network with zero hidden activations stop learning some weights? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: a rectified linear unit inactive for all observed inputs passes zero gradient through that path.

Question 2. What does transfer learning freeze? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: selected pretrained parameters remain fixed; trainable layers or adapters still update. Frozen does not imply unbiased or appropriate for the new task.

Question 3. Is universal approximation a training guarantee? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: no; representational existence says little about finite data, optimization, compute or generalization.
