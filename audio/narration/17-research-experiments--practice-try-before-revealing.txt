Question 1. A paper improves a score after using 10 by more compute. Does it establish a better algorithm? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: it establishes a result under that resource allocation; compare controlled compute and data budgets to isolate efficiency.

Question 2. Why include a negative ablation? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: it shows where an added mechanism fails or adds no value, preventing a universal story from one favorable case.

Question 3. Design a replication with limited compute. Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: reproduce a core mechanism on a small public and synthetic setup, declare deviations, compare to a baseline and avoid claiming full original-scale replication.
