Run learning.py. It trains a tiny logistic model using analytic gradients and checks a gradient numerically. Sigmoid maps a real score into a number between zero and one. Logistic prediction is p equals sigmoid of z equals 1 divided by (1 plus exponential of -z); z equals w dot x plus b. Combining sigmoid with binary log loss yields The derivative of loss with respect to logit z is predicted probability p minus target y, hence The derivative of loss with respect to w is predicted probability p minus target y, multiplied by input x. Derivation: dL and dp equals (p minus y) divided by (p(1 minus p)); dp and dz equals p(1 minus p); the factors cancel. This cancellation is why the training update has a simple form.
