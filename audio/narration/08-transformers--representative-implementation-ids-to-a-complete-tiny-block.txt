For a transparent post-normalization block, use identity output projection, add residual X, then layer-normalize each row: Layer normalization subtracts the mean of vector r, then divides by the square root of its mean squared centered deviation plus epsilon. On the first row r equals (3,0), mean equals 1.5, variance equals 2.25 and epsilon equals 1 times e-5, giving approximately (zero point nine nine nine nine nine eight,negative zero point nine nine nine nine nine eight). Set the feedforward map to The feed-forward output is the rectified linear activation of h times the identity matrix, multiplied by the identity matrix again with zero biases. It yields approximately (zero point nine nine nine nine nine eight,0) on that row. Add its residual and normalize again: the row remains approximately (1,negative 1). These identity choices are educational constants; learned feedforward maps are generally wider and different.

Choose vocabulary projection U equals [[1,0,negative 1],[0,1,0]], shape 2 by 3. A final representation near (1,negative 1) yields vocabulary logits near (1,negative 1,negative 1), whose softmax is approximately (zero point seven eight seven,zero point one zero seven,zero point one zero seven). Selecting or sampling a token from this distribution gives the next identifier; a decoder converts identifiers back to text. The full trace is implemented in attention.py, including the block and logits. It does not train these matrices, so the output has no linguistic capability claim.
