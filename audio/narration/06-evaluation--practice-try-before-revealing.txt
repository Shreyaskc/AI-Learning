Question 1. You improve retrieval recall but answer correctness falls. Explain. Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: extra distractors may overwhelm evidence; reranking, context ordering and generator behavior matter. Diagnose with component ablations.

Question 2. A subgroup has 3 divided by 3 correct outputs. Can you call it solved? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: no; interval uncertainty and sampling selection remain large.

Question 3. Give a release gate for a support assistant. Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: no unauthorized cross-tenant retrieval on adversarial tests, lower confidence bound on supported-answer correctness above an agreed target, ninety fifth percentile latency within budget, escalation path exercised, and staged online rollout.
