Question 1. Is tenant identifier supplied inside a large language model prompt sufficient isolation? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: no; enforce identity and access control list in trusted storage and service layers and test cache and index boundaries.

Question 2. A fairness metric improves while all groups become worse. Can that happen? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: yes; equalizing a rate does not guarantee improving outcomes. Report absolute performance and harm alongside gaps.

Question 3. A model refuses every request. Is it safe and useful? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: it may reduce some harms but fails utility; evaluate the intended task and calibrated refusal rather than a single safety score.
