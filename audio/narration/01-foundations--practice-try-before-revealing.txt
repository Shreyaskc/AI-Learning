Question 1. Is clustering unlabeled support tickets a classifier? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: not by itself; it produces groups without supervised urgency labels. A human mapping groups to categories adds a separate decision step.

Question 2. A model predicts churn using account closed at. What is wrong? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: closure is likely the target or a future consequence. Remove it and audit timestamps and feature lineage.

Question 3. Why retain a test set if cross-validation exists? Pause now and try your answer. When you are ready, continue. Solution: repeated choices using CV scores can overfit those scores; a held-out final set gives a less selection-biased estimate if truly untouched.
