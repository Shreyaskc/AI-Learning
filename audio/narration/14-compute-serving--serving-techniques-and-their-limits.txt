Chapter 14: Training compute, hardware and inference serving. Serving techniques and their limits.

Batching amortizes model execution across requests but can add waiting and mix lengths. Continuous batching lets requests enter and leave generation batches over time. Admission control and per-tenant quotas protect the queue from overload. Prefix caching reuses compatible prompt work; cache correctness depends on model, exact prefix, tokenization and access scope. PagedAttention studies block-based key value memory management. FlashAttention reduces memory traffic for exact attention through input output-aware tiling; it does not abolish the dense attention arithmetic dependency on sequence length.

Speculative decoding proposes tokens using a cheaper draft and verifies them with a target. Correct acceptance procedures can preserve the target sampling distribution under their assumptions. Benefit depends on acceptance rate, draft overhead, batch shape and hardware; verification is not proof that proposed statements are factually true.
